# Career platform references: NeoFly and OnAir

Checked September 19, 2026. These are product design references, not licensed sources for imported jobs, economic statistics, databases, or artwork. Keep OpenCareer's own rules, progression and offline-first single-player architecture.

## NeoFly

[NeoFly's product page](https://www.neofly.net/) describes bush, emergency, airline-pilot and airline-manager paths, 18 mission types, linked campaign chapters, freelance flying, exploration and upgradable home bases.

OpenCareer applications:

- Use its mission breadth as a coverage checklist: bush, cargo, passengers, rescue, sightseeing, advertising/banner, firefighting, humanitarian, interception and airline work. Design each job around OpenCareer's aircraft feasibility, local demand, qualification and flight-lifecycle checks.
- Give unique missions an optional first-time guided walkthrough, and connect related jobs into story arcs without requiring XP grinding.
- Preserve employer careers and ownership-free work alongside company ownership; use base investments only when operations justify their costs.

## OnAir Company

[OnAir's feature overview](https://onair.company/onair-airline-manager/) describes freelance charter and regular-route work, airline management, employees, aircraft acquisition, accounting, scheduled routes, logistics and industry. Its [overview page](https://onair.company/) also describes AI crews, dynamic jobs and persistent multiplayer operations.

OpenCareer applications:

- Support a salaried full-time airline-pilot path and contract work without forcing aircraft purchase or company management.
- Model scheduled routes, staffing, aircraft utilization, wages, fuel, maintenance and accounting as related systems when adding company operations.
- Offer deeper management as an optional progression path, with clear workload and financial constraints. Protect returning players from punishing unattended operations, consistent with OpenCareer's absence rules.
- Use job adaptation to aircraft, range and location as a usability benchmark; continue to require OpenCareer's own runway, capability, access and demand validation.

## Data boundary

Neither product page offers a public dataset or API for importing its economy, mission payouts, flight history or schedules. Do not copy or scrape their private/player data, mission catalogs, formulas, screens or assets. Their public descriptions establish feature coverage, not numeric calibration. Use independently permitted real-world sources and OpenCareer's scenario tests to tune prices, wages and job frequency.
